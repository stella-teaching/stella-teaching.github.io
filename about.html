About Stella My Philosophy My Strengths

I have completed a Graduate Diploma of Early Childhood Education, which has helped me develop a strong understanding of children's learning, development and early childhood teaching practices.

Through my placement experiences, I have observed children, supported daily routines, planned learning experiences and worked alongside other educators. I enjoy seeing children explore, communicate and build confidence through everyday experiences and play.

As I continue developing as an Early Childhood Teacher, I aim to strengthen my planning, observation, communication and inclusive practice while creating a safe environment where every child feels valued.
