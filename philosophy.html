My Teaching Philosophy

I believe children are capable and active learners who learn best when they are given opportunities to explore, make choices and follow their interests. I value play-based learning because play allows children to develop confidence, creativity, communication, and problem-solving skills in a meaningful way.

I also believe secure and respectful relationships are very important in early childhood education. When children feel safe, supported, and respected, they are more confident to participate, communicate and try new experiences. I aim to create an inclusive environment where every child feels valued, respected, and able to participate in their own way.

Families are also important partners in children's learning. I believe working closely with families and listening to their knowledge about their children can help educators provide more meaningful and consistent support for each child's learning, development, and wellbeing.
