Reflecting, Learning and Growing as an Early Childhood Teacher

My professional growth has developed through reflection, collaboration and practical experience during my placements. I continue to learn from children, families and educators, and I use these experiences to improve my teaching practice.

Reflective Practice

Reflection helps me understand what works well in my teaching and what I can improve. After learning experiences, I consider children’s engagement, interests and responses, and think about how I can make future experiences more meaningful.

What I learned

Be flexible and adjust experiences according to children’s responses.

Learning from Feedback

Feedback from supervising educators has supported my professional development. I have learned to listen carefully, accept suggestions and apply feedback to improve my communication, planning and interactions with children.

Evidence

Mentor feedback / placement reflection

Teamwork & Collaboration

Working with other educators helped me understand the importance of communication and teamwork in an early childhood setting. During placement, I supported daily routines, prepared learning environments and discussed children’s learning with educators.

What I developed

Communication, teamwork and confidence.

Family & Inclusive Practice

I understand that children come from different families, cultures and backgrounds. I aim to communicate respectfully with families and create an environment where every child feels welcomed, valued and able to participate.

My focus

Respect, inclusion and responsive practice.

My Ongoing Development

As an emerging early childhood teacher, I am continuing to build my confidence and professional identity. I would like to further develop my intentional teaching, documentation, communication with families and inclusive practice.

  • Documentation
  • Family Communication
